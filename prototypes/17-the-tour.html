  • Billing Refraction: collect or bill

    The vision plan covers the refraction only when the visit bills as a routine exam.

    • Routine exam on the vision plan: it goes through the plan, nothing to collect at the desk.
    • Medical visit: it is not a covered service. Say so at check in, not at checkout.

    The exceptionIf the doctor changes the visit type after the exam, the answer changes with it. Check the encounter before you take payment.

  • Insurance Vision plan or medical: which one bills

    The reason the patient gives on the phone decides which benefit the visit runs on.

    • Due for glasses, no complaint: routine, vision plan.
    • Names a symptom, red eye, pain, flashes, a sudden change: medical, even if they also want glasses.

    The exceptionA patient with a medical diagnosis on file who calls for a routine check still bills medical if the doctor addresses it. Say the plan may not apply before they come in.

  • Scheduling Same day or next available: phone triage

    The short list of what cannot wait for the next open routine slot.

    • Sudden flashes, new floaters, a curtain in the vision: same day, and tell the doctor on duty.
    • Chemical splash: same day, and start them rinsing while they are still on the phone.
    • Gradual blur, no pain, no redness: routine, next available.

    The exceptionIf nothing is open, do not send them away. Take the message to the doctor on duty and call the patient back the same day.

  • Scheduling Which doctor can see this patient

    Not every visit type fits every provider's schedule, so check before you book.

    • Young children and first eye exams go to the providers who take them.
    • Specialty fittings and post op follow ups go back to the provider who started the case.

    The exceptionIf the only opening is with a provider who does not take that visit type, book the right provider later instead of filling the wrong slot.

  • Billing Price lookup: which list is current

    One current list. The old copies in the shared drive and the printed sheet in the drawer are not it.

    • If a printed sheet disagrees with this page, this page wins.
    • If a price is not on the page, say you will confirm and call back. Do not estimate.

    The exceptionPrices reset at the start of the plan year, so a quote given before the reset is not honored after it.

  • Optical Contact lens follow up: what is included

    Follow ups inside the fitting period are part of the fitting fee. After it closes they are a new charge.

    • A recheck inside the fitting period: no new charge, book it as a follow up.
    • A new brand or a power change after the fitting closes: new fitting.

    The exceptionA patient who never came back is still inside the fitting period until it closes. Do not charge them again to finish what they paid for.

  • Recall Recall card: what to say when they call back

    Patients call and ask why they got a card, so answer from the reason code and not from a guess.

    • Routine recall: they are due for their annual exam. Book it.
    • Medical recall: the doctor asked to see them again for something specific. Say that, then book it.

    The exceptionIf the patient says they were already seen, do not argue and do not rebook. Take it to the office manager so the cards stop.

  • Front desk New patient paperwork

    What has to be in hand before the patient goes back.

    • Intake form, signed consent, and a photo of both sides of the card.
    • A patient who arrives with nothing still gets seen. Short form now, the rest at checkout.

    The exceptionIf the name on the card does not match the name on the form, fix it before the visit. Fixing it afterwards is a rebill.

Stop 4 of 10 · The first objection

What is in the knowledge base, and what is not.

It holds your rules, not your charts, and it does not connect to your practice management system.

In the knowledge base

  • Insurance rules and what each plan covers
  • This year's prices, one current list
  • Scheduling and triage protocols
  • Doctor restrictions and who takes what

Not in it, and not connected

  • Patient charts
  • 0 patient records in it
  • Your practice management system

That last one is on purpose: hosted systems do not give an outside vendor database access, so any vendor promising you a nightly sync has not checked yet.
