Product one, live today

Front desk knowledge base

For the practice where every hard question ends up at the office manager's desk.

A searchable knowledge base holding the insurance rules, prices, and protocols the front desk currently keeps in their heads. Short situation pages the team can find mid call, from one search box, instead of a folder to dig through while a patient waits on the line.

Product two, new

Recall done for you

For the practice where recall keeps sliding to next month because the phones were busy.

We run your monthly patient recall for you, using your own recall protocol and your own systems. Not a new process. The one you already wrote down.

Two things make that job fragile. The update step is a checkbox that has to be left unchecked on one run and checked on another, and getting it backwards either double contacts patients or drops them entirely. And it is a monthly job sitting on the same checklist as the daily work, so it is the first thing to slip.

  • The run happens on a schedule instead of when someone finds an afternoon.
  • The call list arrives ready to work, in priority order, with the answers to the questions patients ask when they call back.
  • A monthly report: who was contacted, by which channel, and who booked.
  • The update step gets done, every time, in the right order.
What it does not do

It does not plug into your practice management system. We work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke at any time.

Unlike the knowledge base, recall does involve patient contact information. So it runs inside your own accounts under a signed BAA, and we never touch charts. We will not quote you a recovery rate, because we do not have one to quote. We sell the run and the reliability.

Objections, answered before you have to ask

Every owner asks the same two questions. Here they are, answered straight.

Where does patient data sit

There is no single answer across three products, so we state it per product rather than printing one comfortable line across the whole site.

  • Front desk knowledge base

    0 patient records. It holds rules, prices, and protocols. Not charts. There is nothing in it to breach. Google sign in, restricted to your domain.

  • Recall done for you

    Recall does involve patient contact information. There is no way around that, so it runs inside your own accounts under a signed BAA, with a limited login you create and can revoke. We never touch charts.

  • Practice operations automation

    Time off and payroll are staff information, not patient charts. Scoped to your practice.

Does this connect to our practice management system

No. And that is a decision, not a gap we are hoping you will not notice.

  • Why it does not connect

    Most hosted practice management systems do not give an outside vendor database access. Any vendor promising you a nightly sync into a hosted system is either overselling or has not checked yet. We would rather tell you that now than partway into an integration that was never going to land.

  • What that buys you

    It does not matter which system you run. There is no integration to break when your vendor ships an update, no credentials of ours sitting inside your clinical system, and nothing to unpick if you leave.

  • How recall works without it

    We work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke. You keep the keys. That is the trade, and it is the honest version.
