Questions

The ones we get asked first.

Patient data is answered per product, because the answer really is different per product.
Does the knowledge base hold patient data?

No. 0 patient records. It holds your rules, not your charts.

Does ReExam touch patient data?

Yes, patient contact details. You are the Covered Entity, we are the Business Associate, and the BAA is signed before any data moves. Patient data stays in your systems and we never touch charts.

And the time off and payroll build?

Staff data, not patient data. Time off and payroll, scoped to your practice.

Does any of it connect to our practice management system?

No, and that is on purpose, so it does not matter which one you run. For ReExam we work from a monthly export your team produces, or a limited login you create and can revoke.

We already pay for engagement software. Why add this?

We run alongside it and never require a rip out. Keep your phone system. We will work the list it ignores, on rules tuned to your practice.

Who actually does the work, and where?

Both founders, and one operator works from South Korea. HIPAA permits it and we say so up front rather than waiting to be asked.
