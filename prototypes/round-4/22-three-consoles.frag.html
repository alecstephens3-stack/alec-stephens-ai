01 · Front desk knowledge base

6 example situation pages. Type to filter them.
How soon does this caller need to be seen Lookup · Scheduling

Sudden flashes, new floaters, a curtain across the vision, a chemical splash, or a red painful eye in a contact lens wearer: same day, and tell the doctor on duty. Gradual blur with no pain and no redness: next available.

Exception. If nothing is open, do not send them away. Take it to the doctor on duty and call the patient back the same day.

Which doctor can see this patient Lookup · Scheduling

Young children and first exams go to the providers taking them this week. Specialty fittings and post op follow ups go back to the provider who started the case.

Exception. If the only opening is with a provider who does not take that visit type, book the right provider on a later day. A visit that has to be redone costs more than the wait.

Refraction: collect it or bill it Lookup · Billing

Routine exam on the vision plan: the refraction goes through the plan and there is nothing to collect at the desk. Medical visit: it is not a covered service, so say so at check in and collect at checkout.

Exception. If the doctor changes the visit type after the exam, the answer changes with it. Check the encounter before you take payment.

What is this year's price Lookup · Billing

One current list, the one your office manager last approved. If a printed sheet in a drawer disagrees with this page, this page wins. Self pay and plan prices are different, so ask which one applies before you quote.

Exception. Prices reset at the start of the plan year. A quote given before the reset is not honoured after it.

Vision plan or medical: which one bills Insurance

Due for glasses or an annual check with no complaint: routine, vision plan. Names a symptom, red eye, pain, flashes, a sudden change: medical, even if they also want glasses.

Exception. Both in one visit and the doctor decides which one bills. Do not promise the patient it will be the vision plan.

Charity care and hardship requests Front desk

Take the request in private, away from the waiting room. Write down what was asked for and pass it to the office manager the same day. Do not quote a discount and do not say it is approved.

Exception. Never turn a patient away for asking. Book the visit, note the request, and let the office manager give the answer before the visit.
