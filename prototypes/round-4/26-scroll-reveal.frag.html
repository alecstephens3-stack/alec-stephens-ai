Product 01 · Front desk knowledge base

76 situations. 4 decision lookups. One box.

Live in a 27 person practice, 6 to 7 doctors, one location.

6 example pages. 76 situations mapped, 4 of them decision lookups.

Triage
Decision lookup · How soon do they need to be seen
  • Same day. Sudden flashes, new floaters, a curtain or shadow across the vision.
  • Same day. Chemical splash or something in the eye. Start them rinsing while they are still on the phone.
  • Routine. Gradual blur, no pain, no redness.

If nothing is open, take the message to the doctor on duty. Do not send them away.

Booking
Decision lookup · Which doctor can see this patient
  • Children and first exams. The lookup shows who is taking them this week.
  • Specialty fittings and post op. Back to the provider who started the case.
  • Surgery days. Some providers take no walk in medical visits.

Book the right provider on a later day rather than the wrong slot today.

Billing
Decision lookup · Refraction: collect or bill
  • Routine on the vision plan. It goes through the plan. Nothing to collect at the desk.
  • Medical visit. Not a covered service. Say so at check in, not at checkout.
  • Visit type changed after the exam. The answer changes with it. Check before you take payment.

Tell the patient at check in, so the charge is never a surprise at the desk.

Pricing
Decision lookup · What it costs this year
  • This page wins. If a printed sheet disagrees with it, the sheet is out of date.
  • Self pay and plan prices differ. Ask which one applies before you quote.
  • Not listed? Say you will confirm and call back. Do not estimate.
Plans
Insurance · Vision plan or medical, which one bills
  • Routine, vision plan. Due for glasses or an annual check, no complaint.
  • Medical. They name a symptom: red eye, pain, flashes, a sudden change.
  • Both in one visit. The doctor decides which one bills. Do not promise the plan.
Optical
Front desk · Contact lens follow up
  • Inside the fitting period. Rechecks belong to the fit. Book with the fitting provider.
  • Power changed. New trial first, then the recheck.
  • Supply only. No exam needed if the prescription is current.

Questions

The ones every owner asks.

Patient data is answered per product.
  • Do you hold patient data?

    Knowledge base: no, 0 patient records. ReExam: patient contact details only, under a signed BAA, inside your own systems, never charts. Operations: staff data only, no patients.

  • Does it connect to my practice management system?

    No, and that is on purpose. We work from a list your team exports, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke.

  • We already pay for engagement software.

    Keep it. ReExam runs alongside whatever you have and works the list it ignores. Never a rip out.

  • Where are you, and how fast do you answer?

    Both founders are based in Asia. Urgent issues answered by the next business morning, US time. One ReExam operator works from South Korea, which HIPAA permits.

  • What is the contract?

    Month to month, no term. Keep every page on cancellation, export any time. ReExam cancels monthly, with no setup fee.

  • When do texts and emails go out?

    10am to 7pm in the patient's local time, weekdays. Any reasonable opt out is honoured within 10 business days and kills every message type.
