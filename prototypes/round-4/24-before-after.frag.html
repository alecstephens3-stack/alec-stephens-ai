Questions

The six that decide it.

01
Do you hold patient data?

Per product. The knowledge base holds 0 patient records. ReExam touches patient contact details, so it runs under a signed BAA and the data stays in your systems. Practice operations handles staff records, not patients.

02
Does it connect to my practice management system?

No, and that is on purpose. We work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke.

03
We already pay for engagement software.

Keep it. We run alongside it and work the list it ignores. We never require a rip out.

04
Where is your team?

Both founders are based in Asia. Urgent issues are answered by the next business morning, US time.

05
What happens if we cancel?

Month to month, no term. You keep every page and can export any time.

06
How long until it is live?

30 days for the knowledge base. 10 business days for ReExam from kickoff.
