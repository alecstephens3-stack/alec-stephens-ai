Refraction: collect or bill

The plan covers the refraction only when the visit bills as a routine exam.
  • Routine on the vision plan. It goes through the plan. Nothing to collect at the desk.
  • Medical visit. Not a covered service. Say so at check in, not at checkout.
Exception

If the doctor changes the visit type after the exam, the answer changes with it. Check the encounter before you take payment.

How soon they need to be seen

The short list of what cannot wait for the next open routine slot.
  • Same day. Sudden flashes, new floaters, a curtain in the vision, a chemical splash, a red painful eye in a contact lens wearer.
  • Next available. Gradual blur with no pain and no redness.
Exception

If nothing is open, do not send them away. Take the message to the doctor on duty and call the patient back the same day.

Situation page · Insurance

Vision plan or medical

The reason the patient gives on the phone decides which benefit the visit runs on.
  • No complaint, due for glasses. Routine, vision plan.
  • Names a symptom. Medical, even if they also want glasses.
Exception

Both in one visit: the doctor decides which one bills. Do not promise the patient it will be the vision plan.

Situation page · Optical

Contact lens follow up

Follow ups inside the fitting period are part of the fitting fee.
  • Inside the period. No new charge. Book it as a follow up, not a new exam.
  • New brand or power after it closes. New fitting.
Exception

A patient who never came back for the recheck is still inside the fitting period until it closes. Do not charge them again to finish what they already paid for.
