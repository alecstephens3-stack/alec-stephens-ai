05 · Patient data, per product

The posture, said out loud

The flagship holds nothing. Recall touches contact details, so it runs under a BAA. Those are two different answers and one page should not blur them.
  • Front desk knowledge base. 0 patient records. Insurance rules, prices and protocols only. Google sign in, restricted to your practice domain.
  • ReExam, HIPAA. You are the Covered Entity, we are the Business Associate. The BAA is signed before any patient data moves. Patient data stays in your own systems.
  • ReExam, texting. SMS carries care framed messages only. Anything benefits flavoured goes by email, which is why day 10 is email. Numbers older than 12 months are checked against the Reassigned Numbers Database every campaign.
  • Opt out. Any reasonable opt out is honoured within 10 business days, kills every message type, and propagates both ways with the tool you already run.
  • Send window. 10am to 7pm in the patient's local time, weekdays by default.
  • Where we sit. One operator works from South Korea. HIPAA permits it and we say so before you ask.
  • The list hygiene pass is a deliverable. The first report reads like this: we found 62 duplicates, and 3 people your software has been texting who were never patients.
