03 · Product two

ReExam. Your overdue patient list, worked every month.

A service with software inside, not software. Every month we pull the overdue list from your system, clean it, run the sequence, watch the replies, and hand the owner a 1 page report: contacted, replies, exams booked.
The default sequence · 6 touches over about 6 weeks
It stops the moment a patient books, replies, or opts out.
DAY 0

SMS. You are due for your eye exam.

Book, or reply YES for a call. Your voice, your templates, approved by the owner once.

DAY 3

Email. A short personal note from the doctor.

Same ask, different weight. Some patients answer a doctor and ignore a reminder.

DAY 10

Email only. Most vision plans cover an exam every 12 months.

This is the benefits angle, and benefits talk is marketing adjacent, so it never goes by text. That is a TCPA rule, not a preference. Text carries care framed messages and nothing else.

ComplianceEmail only, every campaign, no exceptions
DAY 17

SMS. A care framed nudge.

Still about the eye, still not about the plan.

DAY 28

SMS. The last text, and it says so.

Telling a patient it is the last one is what makes it the one they answer.

DAY 42

Email. Final note, easy opt out.

Then the patient goes back on the clock and we leave them alone.

Why rules, and not a blast. Exam type intervals for comprehensive, contact lens fitting and medical follow up. Contact lens supply runout, so the message lands when the last box runs dry. January benefits resets. Family batching, so 1 text to a parent books 3 children. Exclusion logic the tools get wrong: deceased, moved, already collected glasses, do not contact. We run alongside whatever you already have and never require a rip out. ReExam is a working name.

And the one everybody asks second.
We do not connect to your practice management system

We work from a list your own team exports, or from inside your system on a limited login you create and can revoke. That is on purpose. Nothing of ours sits between you and your charts, and you can switch us off in a minute without anyone touching your database.

How the messages behave. Text carries care framed messages only. Anything benefits flavoured goes by email, which is why day 10 is email. Numbers older than 12 months are checked against the Reassigned Numbers Database every campaign. The send window is 10am to 7pm in the patient's local time, weekdays by default.
