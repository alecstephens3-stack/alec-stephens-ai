The two questions

Patient data, and what we deliberately do not connect to.

Different products touch different data, so the posture is stated per product.
  • Front desk knowledge base. Holds no patient records. Rules, prices and protocols only.
  • ReExam. Touches patient contact details, so it runs under a signed BAA before any data moves. Your practice is the Covered Entity, we are the Business Associate. Patient data stays in your own systems, and our inboxes hold counts and a link, never patient names.
  • Practice operations automation. Staff scheduling and payroll data. No patient charts.
  • Messaging rules. SMS carries care framed messages only. Numbers older than 12 months are checked against the Reassigned Numbers Database every campaign. Any reasonable opt out is honoured within 10 business days, kills every message type, and propagates both ways with your existing tool. Send window is 10am to 7pm in the patient's local time, weekdays.
  • Where the work happens. One operator works from South Korea. HIPAA permits it and we say so up front rather than waiting to be asked.
The other question

We do not connect to your practice management system. We work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke. Nothing gets ripped out, and nothing of ours can break what you book on.
