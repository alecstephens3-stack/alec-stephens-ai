The two questions every owner asks

Answered per product, because the honest answer is different per product.

Does patient data go into this?

One blanket line across three products would be marketing, not an answer. So here it is one product at a time. We sign a BAA before we start, either way.

Knowledge base

No patient records. It holds your rules, not your charts: insurance policies, pricing, scheduling protocols, procedures. There is nothing in it to breach.

Recall done for you

This one does involve patient contact information. There is no way to run recall without it. So it runs inside your own accounts under a signed BAA, we work from what your protocol needs, and we never touch charts.

Practice operations

Staff records, not patient records. Time off balances and payroll hours, inside your own accounts, scoped to your practice.

Does it connect to our practice management system?

No, and that is on purpose. Most hosted systems do not give an outside vendor database access. Any vendor promising you a nightly sync into a hosted system is either overselling or has not checked yet. We would rather be the ones who checked.

What that means for the knowledge base

It does not matter which system you run. Nothing to integrate, nothing to break when your vendor updates, and no project that stalls waiting on database access nobody will grant.

What that means for recall

We work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login your practice creates and can revoke in a minute. You keep the keys.

What that means for you

We are honest about the seam instead of hiding it. It is the same reason we will not print a recall price we have not set.
