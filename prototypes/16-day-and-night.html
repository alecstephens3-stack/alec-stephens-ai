Product two, the new one

Recall done for you

We run your monthly recall, using your own recall protocol and your own systems.

The job
Scleral letters, medical recall letters across about 10 reason codes, then the calls list and routine postcards once per doctor. At 8 providers that is 8 separate runs.
The trap
The update step is a checkbox left unchecked on one run and checked on another. Backwards, and you either double contact patients or drop them.
What you get
The run on a schedule instead of when someone finds an afternoon. A call list in priority order with the answers patients ask for. A monthly report: who was contacted, by which channel, and who booked.
Honest limit
It does not plug into your practice management system. We work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke.
Patient data
Contact information, never charts. It runs inside your own accounts under a signed BAA.
Price
Priced per practice, on the call. We have not set a list price and we will not guess at one here.
