Question 01 · Front desk knowledge base

What is their plan, and what does this visit run on?

76 situations and 4 decision lookups, live today in a 27 person practice with 6 to 7 doctors. The insurance pages are the ones the team opens first.
What the insurance pages hold
  • Which lookup actually works for that plan: name and date of birth, the subscriber rather than the dependent, or the employer name.
  • What to ask for at booking, so the lookup does not fail while the patient is standing there.
  • Vision plan or medical, and who decides which one the visit bills.
  • How far ahead to pull an authorization, how long it runs, and what to do once it has lapsed.
What we never do
  • No eligibility check. We do not touch a payer, ever.
  • No authorization pulled on your behalf, and none stored.
  • Nothing quoted or promised to a patient about their plan.
  • 0 patient records. The pages hold your rules, not your people.

That line is the point. Tools that talk to plans already exist and some of them are sitting inside the software you already pay for. This is the part none of them cover: what the desk does when the card is not there.
