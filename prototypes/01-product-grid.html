The two questions every owner asks

Answered before you have to ask them.

Two things decide whether this is worth a call. Here they are, without the sales version.

Is this HIPAA trouble?

The honest answer is that it depends on the product, so we set the posture per product instead of making one claim for everything.

  • Front desk knowledge base: no patient data. It holds your insurance rules, prices, and protocols, not your charts. There are 0 patient records in it, so there is nothing in it to breach.
  • Recall done for you: this one does involve patient contact information. It runs inside your own accounts under a signed BAA, with a limited login your practice creates and can revoke. We never touch charts.
  • Practice operations automation: staff time off and pay. No patient data at all.

We sign the BAA before we start either way.

Does this connect to our practice management system?

No, and that is on purpose rather than a gap we are hiding.

  • Most hosted systems do not give an outside vendor database access. Any vendor promising you a nightly sync into a hosted system is either overselling or has not checked yet.
  • Because the knowledge base does not connect to it, it does not matter which system you run. Nothing about your setup blocks the build.
  • For recall, we work from a list your own team runs, or inside your system with a limited login you create and can revoke. You can take that access back in a minute, without calling us.
  • What you give up is automation nobody can honestly deliver. What you get is a build that starts in week one instead of dying in a vendor access request.

Who are we actually hiring?

Two people, and you work with both of us. Alec Stephens spent the last year inside a 27 person eyecare practice. Jusheen Kim was a lead software engineer at J.P. Morgan.

Both founders are based in Asia, so we are not at a desk during your morning rush. Urgent issues are answered by the next business morning, US time. We would rather state the limit than imply a promise we cannot keep.

What if it does not work out?

It is month to month with no term. Cancel and you keep every page, and you can export any time as plain files you can open without us.

You review and approve every page before it goes live, and your doctor signs off on anything clinical. The content is yours. We wrote it down, we do not own it.
